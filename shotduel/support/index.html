Contact us

For questions, bug reports or ideas, write to hello@zuvora.app. For faster help, include your phone model, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and when the problem happened. We reply as soon as we can.

Frequently asked

How do I change the game's language?
The game picks your phone's language automatically. To change it, open Settings, tap the flag and choose a language.

I saw an inappropriate player name.
On the match result screen, or when you pause a match, tap the three-dot button on the rival's badge to report or block the player. We review every report.

Why are there ads?
The game is free and supported by ads. Watching rewarded ads is optional; an ad may sometimes appear at the end of a match.

How do I delete my account and data?
Settings > Delete my account. Details: Account deletion.

If I reinstall the game, do I keep my progress?
Your progress is stored on your phone; uninstalling the game also deletes it.
