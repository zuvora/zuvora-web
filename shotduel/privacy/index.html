ShotDuel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Controller: Zuvora — hello@zuvora.app

Türkçe

1. Summary

ShotDuel needs no account, no e-mail and no phone number. The game works largely offline. When the game first connects to the league, our server gives your phone a random device id and a secret key. The server makes that key from the id and a master key only it holds, so it is not secret from us: the server can work it out again, and uses it to check that match reports really come from your phone.

Data leaves your device only in these cases:

  • When you play an online match: your display name, shirt number, cosmetics, character look and rating go to your opponent and to the matchmaking service. Your IP address is used to make the connection (see 2.2).
  • When an ad is shown: ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and IP address (see 2.3).
  • When you report a player: you send us a report from your own e-mail app (see 2.4).
  • When you take part in the weekly league: your player id, shirt name and number, country (if you chose one) and match results go to Zuvora's league server in Germany (see 2.5).

We do not want your photos or your contacts, we do not ask for your phone's location, and we do not work out a location from your IP address. You should still know that an IP address can reveal an approximate location (usually the city or region).

2. What we collect

2.1 What stays on your phone only

This never leaves your device and is gone when you delete the app.

  • Game progress (level, coins, unlocked cosmetics, records)
  • A device secret (32 bytes the league server made for your phone from its own master key; it stands in for a password)
  • Your recovery code (to get your account back on a new phone)
  • Sound, vibration and language preferences
  • The list of players you have blocked
  • An error log that is never sent automatically; we cannot read it unless you send it to us

2.2 What goes to your opponent and to matchmaking during an online match

Only when you start an online match:

  • Your display name
  • Your shirt number and chosen cosmetics (kit, ball, trail, crest)
  • Your character's look: skin tone, build, hair colour, hair and beard style, boots (in-game appearance only, never a photo)
  • Your player id (a random id; not a name, e-mail or phone number)
  • Your rating (for matchmaking)
  • In an online match, your rating (shown in the game as RP) and the rank level worked out from it are shown to the players you are matched with.
  • Your country, if you chose one (you do not have to)
  • Your IP address and port (to make the connection)

Matchmaking is provided by Photon (Exit Games GmbH, Germany), which sees your IP address to establish the connection. The region is fixed: wherever you play, the game always connects through Photon's Europe region (Amsterdam, the Netherlands). If you play from outside the European Union, this means your data is transferred to the EU.

During a match the two phones first try to connect to each other directly to keep the delay low. To do that each phone receives the other's IP address and port, even if the direct connection then cannot be made, in which case the match runs through Photon's server. The game never shows this address on screen, and we do not collect or store it.

2.3 Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads in the game are served by Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads are entirely optional. You choose to watch one in return for a reward such as extra coins; declining closes no part of the game.
  • An interstitial ad may occasionally be shown between matches. No ad is shown during a match.
  • AdMob uses your device's advertising ID, your IP address and basic device information to serve ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • In the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form is shown before any personalised ad; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Account → Privacy choices. Elsewhere the game requests non-personalised ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.4 Reporting and blocking players

When you report a player's name, your e-mail app opens with a ready-made report to hello@zuvora.app: the reported name, player id, match id and time. Sending it is up to you; if you do, we use your e-mail address and the report only to review inappropriate content. The players you block are kept on your phone only.

2.5 League server

Since 30 October 2026 the weekly league runs on Zuvora's own server (shotduel-api.zuvora.app), hosted by Hetzner Cloud in Germany (EU). Its database is backed up continuously to Cloudflare R2 storage restricted to the EU. The first time the app connects to the league it registers your phone; after every online match both phones send the server a signed match report. The server stores:

  • Player record: a random player id (not a name, e-mail or phone number), your shirt name and number, your country if you chose one, your league rating, match and win counts, and fair-play counters (matches whose two reports disagreed or arrived from one side only, a review flag, a temporary ban if one was applied)
  • Device record: a random device id, a SHA-256 hash of the app's random install id (the install id itself is not stored), platform, app version, the IP address the phone registered from, and when the device was first and last seen
  • Match reports and results: match id, both players' ids, score, result and how the match ended, whether it was ranked, the friend-room code if there was one, ratings before and after, and when the report arrived
  • Weekly standings and rewards: league, points, wins, goals, final rank, and coin or badge rewards

The IP address is used to limit how many registrations can come from one address (abuse prevention). It is saved once, at registration, and is not used to work out a location. No e-mail, phone number, advertising ID or location is sent to this server. Your shirt name, number, country and weekly points are shown to other players on the leaderboard.

When the account is created. In the store version of the game, when you type a username on the first-launch name card, the game asks the server whether the name is free; that needs an account, so the player and device record listed above (including the IP address at registration) is created at that moment, even before you play a match. If this installation already created a record, that one is used and no new one is made; the server recognises the installation by an irreversible digest (SHA-256) of the random installation ID made on the phone and keeps that digest on the device record, not the ID itself.

Username. When you claim a username, the server keeps the name as you typed it and a folded upper-case form used to tell look-alike names apart (e.g. "Şahin" → "SAHIN"). You are given a member number starting with "SD-", which is stored. The time you first claimed a name (your member-since date) and the time you last changed it (for the rename limit) are kept. Your username is shown on the league table and to your opponents; the same name (in its folded form) and the same member number are never given to two players. Suggested names are held for you for 10 minutes: the suggested name and your player ID are recorded for that time and the record is deleted when it ends. When you change your name or delete your account, the old name stays in a name history together with your player ID (the name's forms, when it was claimed and when it was released) and is not given to anyone else for 30 days; a name you changed away from can be given back only to you during that time. The name history is kept for as long as your account exists.

Name reports. When you report an opponent's name with REPORT, the server records the reporting and the reported player's IDs, the reported player's username at that moment and the time of the report. Zuvora reviews reports; the time and outcome of the review (e.g. the name being changed) may be written to the same record. A player can report the same opponent once in 24 hours and at most 20 times a day. Report records are kept until either side deletes their account.

Account recovery. Your recovery code is shown only to you; the server does not store the code itself, only its irreversible digest (SHA-256) and the times the code was created, renewed and revoked. When you recover your account with the code, renew the code, or your old phone takes the account back, an event record is written: the time, the kind of event and its outcome, a keyed one-way digest of your IP address (not the address itself), and, for a take-back, the old device's ID. A successful recovery and a successful take-back also create a new device record for the phone that gets the account; unlike the event record, that record keeps your IP address itself, as registration does. In failed recovery attempts your IP address itself is used only in the server's memory for the attempt limit and is not written to the database. Failed recovery attempts that match no account are recorded the same way, without a player ID, to monitor abuse. If your code is used on another phone, your old phone can take the account back within 7 days; the old device record keeps when that period ends. The account moves to this phone the moment your code is accepted, and its credentials are written to the phone; closing the confirmation page does not undo this. When you close the page, the account's username becomes this phone's player name; this phone's shirt number and rating stay as they are (the account's number and rating are taken with the continue button). If this phone already has an account (for example because you typed a name on the first-launch card) and you take another account with a recovery code, the phone's earlier account record (the player and device record, the IP address at registration, and its username, member number and recovery code digest if it had them) stays on the server: the phone now holds only the recovered account's credentials, so deleting the account in the game does not delete that record.

The copy on your phone. When the account on this phone is revoked for good (for example when the old phone you recovered it from takes it back within 7 days, or the account is deleted), the server says so in reply to the phone's next request; the phone deletes that account's credentials, username, member number, member-since date and recovery code from this phone, and your player name too if it is that account's name. Your flag, crest, look, installation ID and game progress stay on the phone; the name card opens to claim a name again, and the next name check creates an account as on a first launch. The game reads the time in the server's replies and keeps the difference from your phone's clock only in memory while the app is open, never saved; a request refused because your clock is far off is sent once more with that difference. Nothing extra is stored on the phone or on the server for this.

Deletion. When you delete your account in the game, your username is removed from your profile at once, your recovery code is revoked at once (it no longer opens any account) and your name holds are deleted; your old name is not given to anyone else for 30 days. When the 30 days are up, your member number, name history, recovery code digest, recovery events and every name report you are a party to are erased completely together with your player record.

2.6 Never collected

Photos · camera · microphone · location (neither your phone's location nor one worked out from your IP address) · contacts · SMS · phone number · payment details. We only get your e-mail address if you write to us yourself.

3. Children

ShotDuel is for ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think your child has left data with us, write to hello@zuvora.app and we will delete it.

4. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK art. 11)

  • Deletion. Deleting the app deletes everything held on your phone. Settings → Delete my account deletes your username, coins and progress on the phone and, on the league server, immediately removes your shirt name and country, your weekly standings and rewards, the IP address your phone registered from and the install-id hash, and permanently disables your phone's sign-in key; you may also write to hello@zuvora.app. It cannot be undone.
  • What is kept after deletion. For fraud prevention the league server keeps, for at most 30 days, under the random ids only and with no name attached: your player record's rating and match counters, your match reports and results, and the device record (random device id, platform, app version). 30 days after deletion the server erases these automatically. A match you played also belongs to your opponent: their copy of the result stays, but your id is removed from it. If you want this to happen sooner, write to hello@zuvora.app.
  • Access and correction. You may request access to, or correction of, anything held for you.
  • Objection. Choosing not to play online means nothing is sent to matchmaking; you can change your ad choices in Settings.

Contact: hello@zuvora.app

5. Changes

If this policy changes, its date is updated, and important changes are announced in the app.
