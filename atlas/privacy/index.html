Turtle Atlas is a free mobile puzzle game by Zuvora. This page explains what happens to your data when you play.

1. In short

  • Turtle Atlas needs no account, no sign-in, no e-mail and no phone number. There is no chat and nothing to buy.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. We run no servers of our own and receive no data about you.
  • Apart from the store's own rating window (section 7), the only part of the game that sends data from your device is the ad service, AppLovin MAX. It shows a video ad only when you choose to watch one for a reward, but it starts with the game and receives device data such as your advertising ID and IP address (section 3).
  • Reminders are created on your device. A postcard you share leaves the game only when you choose where to send it.

2. What stays on your device

The following is saved in the game's own storage on your device (Unity PlayerPrefs) and is never sent to us:

  • game progress, stars and records; Daily Island results and your streak; pearls, tickets and unlocked decorations;
  • settings: music, sound effects, vibration, language;
  • a skill estimate worked out from your recent results, used only to decide when to offer Atlas's help;
  • the start times of your recent play sessions and which reminder was last scheduled, used only to time reminders;
  • how many ads you watched today (there is a daily limit) and when the rating window was last requested, so the game does not ask too often;
  • the postcard image you last created for sharing.

This data stays until you clear the game's data or uninstall the game. If you have device backup turned on (for example Android backup or iCloud), your operating system may include it in your own backup, which Google or Apple handle under your account; we cannot access it.

3. Ads: AppLovin MAX

3.1 When you see ads

You can choose to watch a short video ad for a reward: extra moves when you run out, "try from here" after a loss, or repairing your daily streak. An ad starts only after you tap to watch it. There are no banner or pop-up ads. Watching an ad is always optional: you can keep playing without it.

3.2 Who provides them

Ads are provided through AppLovin MAX, a service of AppLovin Corporation (United States). The AppLovin software built into the game starts when the game opens and loads ads in advance, so it can send data to AppLovin even if you never watch an ad.

3.3 What AppLovin may collect

According to AppLovin's privacy policy, this can include:

  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and App Set ID; on iPhone and iPad, the identifier for vendors (IDFV), and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address, and the approximate location (such as your country) that can be worked out from it;
  • device information: make, model, operating system, settings such as language and time zone, and mobile carrier;
  • information about the game and your session: app name and version, installation and session information, performance;
  • your interaction with ads: which ads were shown, and whether you watched to the end or tapped them.

3.4 What it is used for

AppLovin uses this data to choose, show and measure ads, to confirm that you earned the reward, to prevent fraud and keep its service secure, and to give us reports. AppLovin may also use it for its own purposes, including personalised (interest-based) advertising and improving its services. The details, and AppLovin's own privacy choices, are in AppLovin's privacy policy: https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/

3.5 What we receive

From AppLovin we receive only summary reports, for example how many ads were shown and what they earned. We do not receive your name or contact details, and we do not try to find out who individual players are. We do not sell your data for money.

3.6 Other ad networks

Through MAX, AppLovin can also request ads from other ad networks ("mediation"). At the moment no other networks are enabled in this game; if any are added, they will be listed here with links to their privacy policies before that version is released.

4. Your ad choices

  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID (on older versions "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"; menu names vary by device). Apps can then no longer read the ID.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game can read the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when your device asks. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • AppLovin's own tools for opting out of interest-based ads and for accessing your data (including an app to view or erase data linked to your advertising ID) are described under "Manage Your Privacy Choices" in AppLovin's privacy policy.
  • After opting out you will still see an ad when you choose to watch one; it will just be less tailored to you.
  • Uninstalling the game stops all collection by the game and by the AppLovin software.

5. Reminders

The game can schedule reminders on your device, for example when the Daily Island is ready, when your streak is at risk, or when you have not played for a few days. They are created and stored by your device's operating system: there is no notification server and no push token, and nothing about them is sent to us or anyone else. The game picks the time on your device, based on when you usually play. On iOS, and on Android 13 and later, reminders appear only if you allow notifications; on any device you can turn them off at any time in the system settings.

6. Sharing

When you tap Share, the game opens your device's own share menu with a postcard image and a short text (for example your Daily Island emoji card and, once the game is in the store, a link to its store page). The image is created on your device. Nothing leaves the game until you pick an app or a contact in that menu; what you share is then handled by that app under its own privacy policy. The store link only carries tags showing that it came from a shared card, not who shared it. The game does not ask for access to your photos; if you choose to save the image, your device may ask for permission. Copying the card to the clipboard stays on your device.

7. Rating requests

After a good win, the game may ask Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS) to show their own rating window. Google or Apple decide whether to show it, and any rating or review you leave is handled by them under their policies and published with your store account like any other review. The game is not told whether you rated it; it only remembers on your device when it last asked, so it does not ask too often.

8. Never collected

Photos · camera · microphone · your device's location (GPS) · contacts · SMS · e-mail address · phone number · payment details.

9. Children

Turtle Atlas is made for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has played the game, write to hello@zuvora.app; we hold no player data ourselves, but we will help you ask AppLovin to delete the data linked to that device.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Turkey's personal data law KVKK, article 11, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to ask what personal data is processed about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use for personalised advertising. Some US state laws treat letting an ad network use device data for personalised ads as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal data; the settings in section 4 limit this use.

We keep no data about you on our side, so we cannot look anything up by your name. For data held by AppLovin, use AppLovin's tools or contact AppLovin directly (see its privacy policy). For any question, write to hello@zuvora.app; we reply within 30 days.

11. Where data is processed

AppLovin is based in the United States and processes data on servers in the United States and other countries. If you play from Turkey, this means the data is transferred outside Turkey.

12. Security

The game runs no servers of our own and we keep no player database. Data collected by AppLovin is protected by AppLovin's own security measures, described in its privacy policy.

13. Changes

If the game changes (for example if new ad networks, analytics, purchases or accounts are added), we will update this page and its date before the new version is released, and mention significant changes in the store release notes.
